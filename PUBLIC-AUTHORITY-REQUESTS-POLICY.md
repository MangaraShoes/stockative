# Public authority data requests: internal policy

**Controller:** Mangara Shoes BV (Belgium), operator of Stockative
**Owner:** Patricia Cossettin
**Adopted:** 30 September 2026

This is the process declared in the Meta App Review "Data handling" section (requests-4). It covers any request from a public authority (police, courts, regulators, national security bodies, in any country) for personal data that Stockative holds about merchants or their connected accounts. That includes Platform Data received from Meta, Shopify, Pinterest, TikTok or Google.

Requests received to date: none.

## 1. Legality review (required)
- Requests are accepted only in writing, from an identifiable authority. Before anything is disclosed, the owner confirms who sent the request, its legal basis and its scope.
- If the request is not plainly valid under Belgian and EU law (GDPR, Belgian procedural law), a Belgian lawyer is consulted before any response is sent.
- Requests from authorities outside the EU are only answered through a legal basis recognised in the EU, such as a mutual legal assistance treaty or an EU/Belgian court order.

## 2. Challenging unlawful requests
- A request that is unlawful, overbroad or lacks a valid legal basis is refused or challenged, with legal advice, and is not complied with voluntarily.
- Where the law allows, the affected merchant is told about the request before any disclosure.

## 3. Data minimisation
- Only the minimum data strictly needed to satisfy a valid request is disclosed. The scope is narrowed wherever possible (specific account, specific period, specific fields).
- Access tokens and app secrets are never disclosed.

## 4. Documentation
Every request is logged, including requests that are refused. The log records:
- date received and the requesting authority;
- the stated legal basis;
- the data requested;
- the decision and its legal reasoning;
- who was consulted, including the lawyer;
- what was disclosed, if anything, and when;
- whether the merchant was notified.

The log is kept for at least 5 years.
